# Community guide

Keep Mensa recognizable. Use the included sheet for your selected style. Give each short scene one clear idea and let the absurdity play naturally.

Review the face, tabletop shape, four foot-ended supports, props, and continuity before animating. No extra arms or hands. If the model misses one detail, ask for that repair instead of restarting everything.

When sharing, describe work as community-created. Suggested credit: “Made with the Mensa Community Engine by Nosimaj Media.” Do not imply a real person or organization endorsed a fictional scene. Keep trading claims out of ordinary creative work.

Feedback is useful: share the idea, selected style, provider, references used, and what went wrong. Avoid private information and account credentials.
