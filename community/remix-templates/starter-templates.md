# Starter ideas

- One PS2 picture: Mensa waits alone beside a closed roadside cafe in the rain.
- Classic cinematic: Mensa carries one cup through a storm without spilling it. Six shots, no music.
- Late-Z H3 R2V: Mensa braces beneath a floating boulder, then rises into a white aura. 15 seconds, 16:9, no music.
- Fictional ad: a solemn furniture showroom demonstrates a table that walks itself into place.
- Quiet loop: Mensa stays still under fluorescent lights; one small indicator blinks nearby.
- Episode: Mensa delivers a fragile object across an increasingly strange empty city. Start with board one only.

These are optional community-made ideas, not official lore. Change any setting, mood, prop, or action you like. Keep the flat body and four feet unless you deliberately want a character variant.
