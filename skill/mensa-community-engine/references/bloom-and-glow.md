# PS2 light and glow

Use simple flat/vertex light and crude contact shadows by default. Keep warm-brown skin, black hair, teeth, eyes, and the flat top readable. Glow is optional, never mandatory decoration.

When requested, use restrained low-resolution sprite halos, a soft era-appropriate sky overexposure, or simple additive effects. Do not bleach Mensa's skin into a white silhouette. Reject glossy PBR skin, realistic subsurface scattering, ray-traced reflections, volumetric god rays, and dense cinematic particles.

Late-Z replaces this layer with hard-edged opaque cel effects. A glow does not authorize a hybrid style. One major light/material upgrade fails the PS2 gate; repair only lighting/material behavior while preserving composition and identity.
