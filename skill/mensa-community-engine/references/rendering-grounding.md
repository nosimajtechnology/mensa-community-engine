# Original-PS2 rendering grounding

Before every flagship PS2 image, board, or repair, resolve one primary original-platform build; visually inspect 3-5 authentic gameplay/in-engine screenshots; assign each a narrow role; derive a scene rendering contract; then apply the identity and fidelity gate. Reuse inspected sources in an unchanged project when their roles still fit.

Default: original **Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas, PS2 (2004)**. Its low-poly skin/face construction, diffuse texture allocation, simple light, mundane urban environments, and native capture softness fit Mensa. This does not import CJ, game factions, dialogue, costumes, locations, or story canon. A user-named different PS2 game may control an appropriate setting without upgrading the character.

Reject Definitive Edition, remasters, PC/Xbox/mobile ports, mods, texture packs, ReShade, pre-rendered cinematics, fan art, and platform-ambiguous captures. Prefer platform-labeled archives and contemporary captures. Never redistribute game screenshots or extracted models with this skill.

## Reference roles

- Selected Mensa PS2 sheet: identity, silhouette, tabletop and support construction.
- Source meme if needed: facial likeness and original anatomy only, not photographic rendering.
- Gameplay A: angular head/limb density and diffuse skin texture.
- Gameplay B: floor/building massing, props, draw distance, scene density.
- Gameplay C: vertex-like light, shadow limits, atmosphere, camera.
- Approved project image: current scene and continuity.

## Rendering contract

Native-looking 4:3 and 480i-era capture behavior unless user requests another ratio. Low polygon counts, broad faceted planes, chunky hair mass with painted curls, painted facial features, small diffuse textures, low-detail feet/toes, limited UV detail. Flat/vertex/Gouraud-like shading and simple contact/blob/projected shadows. Sparse props, repeated NPC assets, low-resolution tiled environments, fog-managed distance, simple alpha weather/effects. No HUD by default.

The table is continuous skin-colored geometry, not wood. Do not use excessive triangle mottling as a substitute for true sparse mesh construction. Do not paste a photoreal head onto a low-poly body.

Reject high-poly smoothing, realistic skin pores, PBR, ray tracing, glossy studio highlights, global illumination, bokeh/DOF, volumetric fog, dense particles, modern mocap, or retro filters over modern graphics. Details must stay legible without becoming a modern beauty render.
