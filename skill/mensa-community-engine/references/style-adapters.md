# Style adapter router

Choose a mode first; then ask style only if unresolved. Default/PS2/flagship/continue after chooser selects FLAGSHIP PS2. A named or approved style-specific image skips the chooser.

| Style | Aliases | Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FLAGSHIP PS2 | PS2, default, original-console | [rendering-grounding.md](rendering-grounding.md), [bloom-and-glow.md](bloom-and-glow.md) |
| LATE-Z BATTLE CEL | Late-Z, 90s DBZ-esque, Buu-saga-inspired | [late-z-battle-cel.md](style-adapters/late-z-battle-cel.md) |

Record style ID/version. Use the source for underlying identity and selected sheet for translation. A style changes paint, line, geometry treatment, camera, and cadence; it cannot change tabletop construction, foot count, or likeness. Do not mix PS2 and cels unless requested.

Preserve selected style through first frame, boards, animation, and repair. Label stages `GENESIS FRAME · FLAGSHIP PS2`, `STORYBOARD · LATE-Z BATTLE CEL`, or corresponding stage/style. Motion references control motion only, not identity or audio.
