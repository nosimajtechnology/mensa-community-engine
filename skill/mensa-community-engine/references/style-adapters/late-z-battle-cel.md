## Rendering lock

- Mid-1990s television-cel look, 4:3 unless another ratio is requested.
- Confident dark ink contours; thicker outer silhouette, sparse interior marks.
- Warm brown flat skin paint with two opaque shadow values and occasional restrained third highlight; hard edges, no airbrushing or soft body gradients.
- Preserve recognizable grinning face, cheek structure, narrow sidelong eyes and high-top black curls. Hair is a compact black shape with economical curl marks, not a modern high-detail afro render.
- Flat rectangular top, exactly four foot-ended supports, no hands or added arms. Supports may be simplified in ink without becoming standard humanoid limbs.
- Hand-painted environments with broad opaque shapes and sparse terrain lines; stable fine cel-photography grain, restrained softness and minute color bleed.
- No modern glossy digital finish, heavy VHS damage, scanlines, film burns, 3D shading, PBR, bokeh, volumetric glow, or franchise symbols.

## Effects and camera

Aura uses opaque flame planes mostly behind the body, sparse foreground arcs, and preserved warm-brown paint. No canonical aura color or signature attack exists in this engine; choose per scene. For sustained aura requests, retain it through payoff with lower intensity as needed.

Use expressive eye/foot/table-edge inserts, low three-quarter tension, wide reveals, practical pans, short push-ins, hard cuts, and brief impact cels. No constant camera movement or fluid modern interpolation. Give each shot one dominant motion channel. Stable held ink/paint/grain, then decisive brief bursts.

## State changes and action

POWER_UP: intact four-foot stance -> one progressing environmental cue -> brief effect/impact bridge -> intact completed state -> readable aftermath. Keep body rectangular. Do not add fists, upright shoulders, or transformed franchise hair.

IMPACT: approach -> one clear foot/body contact -> short impact insert -> displaced target -> aftermath. Contact cannot remain a multi-second static overlap. Requested complex combat is staged in separated beats, not silently replaced with a power-up.

Repair the smallest failure: restore anatomy and face; harden cel boundaries if painterly; simplify hair/contours if too modern; separate contact if limbs duplicate; put aura behind body if identity disappears. Preserve requested action, effects color, and duration.
